HTTP HANDLER LAYER (server/internal/httpapi/kosync.go) - NOT FUZZED
- The handlers wrap the body with http.MaxBytesReader (register 4096, auth 1024, put 16384), bind with gin
  ShouldBindJSON and map errors to statuses (400 / 401 / 402 / 500). None of that is covered by a fuzz target
  or by this PR's tests. UNCONFIRMED: that the status mapping and the body limits behave as intended under
  adversarial input; they are outside HRD-070's service-level scope and need their own item.
- The kosync fuzz decode was changed to json.NewDecoder(...).Decode(&p) to mirror ShouldBindJSON (first JSON
  value only, trailing data accepted) instead of json.Unmarshal. Equivalence with gin's binder is read from
  gin's source (json.API default = encoding/json), not measured here: UNCONFIRMED.
